Welcome to Hetchy

Hetchy turns chat conversations into shipped pull requests. Let's get your workspace ready in three quick steps.

  • 1Name your organization. It groups your repos, integrations, and teammates.
  • 2Connect your integrations. Link GitHub, paste your Anthropic key, and add Slack so the bot can post and open PRs.
  • 3Start shipping. Mention the bot in a channel or open a chat to kick off your first task.
